# COI review (placeholder)

This skill is temporary. Replace this file with the real Certificate of Insurance review instructions. The upload and review flow will pick up the new file on the next deploy.

Until then, read the uploaded PDF and write a short plain-language summary:

- Who is insured
- Who is listed as additional insured or certificate holder, if present
- Policy effective and expiration dates
- Coverage types and limits you can see
- Anything that looks missing or unclear

Do not invent policy details that are not in the document. If a field is not visible, say so.
